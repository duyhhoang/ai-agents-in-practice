PHOTO ID: PHO-SITE004-INCID-001
Date: 31 August 2025
Site: SITE-004, Vienna South (AT-A2-KM18 motorway services)
Unit: EVC-2024-0008 (AC-22kW)
Stage: Incident response – connector damage
Caption: Connector assembly of EVC-2024-0008 removed for inspection following incident report IR-SITE004-2025-0031. Photo taken by field technician Stefan Krone at 17:22. Discolouration and charring visible on contact pins 1 and 3 (L1 and PE conductors). Burn pattern is consistent with arc tracking rather than sustained overcurrent. Housing of connector body shows melting and blackening on interior face. Replacement part #KKE-2024-Rev2 visible in background, still in packaging. External cable (vehicle side, not EuroCharge property) also visible – shows corresponding burn marks on receptacle, suggesting vehicle connector was the initiation point.
