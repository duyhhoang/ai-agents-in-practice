R1 – Plastic parts: Sea freight dependency – long lead time (MEDIUM / MEDIUM)
  Status update: No disruption events in 2024. Customs hold (SHIP-2024-0008, January 2024) resolved within 4 days; documentation process updated to prevent recurrence.
  Actions since last review:
    – European regional supplier qualification (Plastipak GmbH, Munich) reached prototype stage. Volume qualification Q2 2025.
    – HS code pre-classification letter obtained from DE Zoll for EVC plastic parts → reduces future customs query risk.
  Residual risk: MEDIUM / LOW (improved from MEDIUM / MEDIUM)
  Next review: Q2 2025

R4 – Final assembly: Missing sub-assembly blocks complete product (HIGH / HIGH)
  Status update: Assembly readiness checklist introduced November 2024. Zero assembly stoppages due to missing sub-assembly since introduction.
  Actions: Checklist embedded in ERP production order confirmation step (mandatory GR confirmation for all sub-assemblies before PROD order status changes to REL).
  Residual risk: LOW / HIGH (likelihood reduced)
  Next review: Q3 2025

R5 – Testing: Failed VDE test causes rework delay (MEDIUM / HIGH)
  Status update: NCR-2025-0088 (crimp failure, cable harness) – resolved. Pre-assembly harness pull-test added to in-process inspection (from batch CBL-F-2025-025 onwards).
  In-process pre-test results since introduction: 0 defects in 4 subsequent batches.
  Residual risk: LOW / HIGH → propose downgrade to LOW / MEDIUM at next review if trend holds.
  Next review: Q2 2025

R7 – Installation: Concrete base delay (MEDIUM / HIGH)
  No new events. Standard operating procedure updated: site readiness inspection (ref. SITE-INSP checklist) now mandatory sign-off before unit is dispatched from Budapest.
  Residual risk: LOW / HIGH

R8 – Use phase: Standby energy dominates operational impact (HIGH / MEDIUM)
  Product engineering confirmed firmware v2.1.6 reduces standby power on DC-50kW units by approximately 8% (from 120 W to 110 W) through improved sleep-mode logic. Operator M confirmed OTA update rollout to all deployed units by end Q1 2025.
  Design target for DC-150kW next generation: <200 W standby (current: 280 W).
  Residual risk: HIGH / MEDIUM – no change (structural; requires product redesign for significant improvement)
