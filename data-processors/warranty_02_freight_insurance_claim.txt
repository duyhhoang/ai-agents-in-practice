DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT
Prior to departure from Budapest, driver M. Horváth identified a compression mark (approximately 15 × 8 cm) on the top-right corner of the outer carton of Unit 2 of 3 (unit EVC-2024-0009, serial SN-0010F441). The inner packaging foam was partially compromised. The damage was not present when units were loaded from the assembly facility (loading inspection photo PHO-FACTORY-SHIP-009-A confirms intact packaging at departure). The damage is believed to have occurred during storage in the TransLogistics EU Hamburg transit warehouse between 14 March (inbound from Hamburg port) and 20 March 2024 (pre-departure loading).

DAMAGE ASSESSMENT
EuroCharge Quality team opened packaging on 20 March 2024 and inspected unit EVC-2024-0009:
  – External housing: 2 scratches on rear panel (approx. 8 mm and 12 mm length, surface only)
  – Display interface: No damage observed ✓
  – Electronic modules: Visual inspection normal ✓
  – Connector: No damage ✓
  – Electrical safety test (partial, bench): Insulation resistance >2 MΩ; earth continuity 0.07 Ω ✓

Assessment result: Unit is cosmetically damaged but functionally intact. Customer (Installation Partners EU, SITE-002) has been consulted. They will accept the unit with a price reduction of EUR 350 (agreed via email 21 March 2024) to account for the cosmetic defect.
