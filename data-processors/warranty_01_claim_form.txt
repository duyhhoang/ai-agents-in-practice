DESCRIPTION OF FAULT
On 30 August 2025, user reported an unusual high-pitched buzzing sound and burning smell emanating from the charging unit during an active charging session. The user disconnected immediately. Site staff confirmed unit offline. Field technician attended within 45 minutes and identified burn marks and arc tracking on the connector assembly (contact pins 1 and 3). The charging unit was taken out of service.

The fault resulted in:
  – Replacement of the connector assembly (part #KKE-2024-Rev2)
  – Emergency field technician call-out (unplanned, outside normal maintenance cycle)
  – Unit downtime: 30 August (16:45) – 31 August 2025 (09:00) = approximately 16 hours

OPERATOR STATEMENT
We acknowledge that the root cause investigation (ref. NCR field report) indicates the primary initiation was likely a user inserting a damaged vehicle-side connector. However, the EuroCharge unit did not prevent the subsequent arc event or provide an alarm before the user noticed the fault. We are claiming under warranty for the component replacement and emergency response costs, as the unit was within warranty and no product design exclusion appears to apply. We reserve the right to request further analysis if the warranty claim is not accepted.
