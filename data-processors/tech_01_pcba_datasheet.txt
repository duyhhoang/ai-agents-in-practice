1. PRODUCT OVERVIEW
The EVC-CTRL-22A is a single-board charging controller assembly designed for integration into the EuroCharge EV charging station product family. It manages pilot signal generation, communication protocol handling (OCPP 1.6J / 2.0.1), safety monitoring, and user authentication (RFID ISO 14443-A/B). The board is conformal-coated for protection against condensation and humidity in outdoor environments (IP54 enclosure assumed).

2. ELECTRICAL SPECIFICATIONS
  Supply voltage:            24 VDC ± 10% (from internal SMPS)
  Supply current (typical):  420 mA
  Supply current (max load): 780 mA
  Pilot signal output:       1 kHz, ±12 V square wave, per IEC 61851-1
  Pilot signal duty cycle:   Variable, 10%–96% (corresponds to 6 A–80 A current limit)
  RFID reader:               13.56 MHz, ISO 14443-A/B, ISO 15693
  RFID read range:           ≥ 5 cm (tested with standard MIFARE Classic card)
  Communication:             Ethernet 10/100 Mbps (RJ45), 4G LTE CAT-M1 (optional module)
  OCPP:                      1.6J (JSON over WebSocket), 2.0.1 ready (firmware selectable)
  Operating temperature:     -40°C to +70°C (board), -25°C to +50°C (system)
  Storage temperature:       -55°C to +85°C
  Humidity (non-condensing):  5%–95% RH

8. KNOWN ISSUES AND FIELD NOTES
  Rev. 4.0 → 4.1 change: Added pull-up resistor R142 (10 kΩ) on pilot signal feedback line. Resolves rare false "EV disconnected" event during noisy grid conditions. Field retrofit possible but not required for Rev. 4.0 units already in service.
  Rev. 4.1 → 4.2 change: Firmware v2.1.6 adds watchdog timer reset improvement (addresses OCPP session freeze reported in field, ~0.2% occurrence on v2.1.4). OTA firmware update available via OCPP ChangeConfiguration command.
