INCIDENT REPORT - EVC-2024-0008
Date Filed: 2025-08-31
Reported by: Site Operator Maria Santos, SITE-004
Equipment ID: EVC-2024-0008

INCIDENT DESCRIPTION:
During routine 2-hour charging session on 2025-08-30 around 16:45, user reported charger making unusual high-pitched buzzing sound and slight burning smell. User immediately unplugged vehicle.

INVESTIGATION:
Field technician Stefan Krone arrived on-site within 45 minutes.

Findings:
1. Connector assembly showed discoloration and charring on pin contacts
2. Burn pattern consistent with arc tracking (possible connection resistance issue)
3. Visual inspection: Customer had previously inserted vehicle connector in reverse orientation (attempted before trip)
4. This caused partial contact and intermittent arc during charging

ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS:
- User error: Connector inserted incorrectly (design allows manual insertion, but strain caused partial seating)
- The charging session before incident (same day morning) may have caused micro-arcs that degraded contacts
- No fault detected in charger electronics or power delivery (current limiting works correctly)

CORRECTIVE ACTION TAKEN:
1. Connector assembly replaced with new unit (part #KKE-2024-Rev2)
2. Full electrical safety retest performed - all parameters OK
3. Site operator re-trained on proper connector insertion technique
4. Updated user manual with clearer diagrams (now includes warning about reverse insertion)
5. QR code instructions updated with video showing correct insertion

PREVENTIVE MEASURES:
- Design improvement: Next generation connector (in design phase) will use keyed design to prevent reverse insertion
- Customer communication: Email sent to all users about connector care

RISK ASSESSMENT: LOW
Equipment functioned as designed. User error, easily prevented through better design.

Status: RESOLVED
