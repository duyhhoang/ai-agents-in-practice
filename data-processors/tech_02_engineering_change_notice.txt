TITLE
Replace DEHP-containing PVC cable insulation with DEHP-free compound in Cable Harness Assembly (MAT-CABL-HARN)

BACKGROUND AND REASON FOR CHANGE
The REACH compliance review conducted in January 2024 (ref. REACH-EVC-2024-001) identified Bis(2-ethylhexyl) phthalate (DEHP), CAS 117-81-7, in the PVC insulation of certain cables within the harness assembly supplied by Certified Cable Works (A6). DEHP concentration was measured at 0.15% by weight in the PVC compound, exceeding the 0.1% SVHC disclosure threshold under REACH Article 33.

Although current use is not prohibited (DEHP appears on REACH Annex XIV with sunset date but with relevant authorisations in place for specific cable applications), EuroCharge has committed to eliminating DEHP from all products by end of 2025 as part of its chemical management programme. Additionally, several major fleet customers (including EuroCharge Network Operator M) have contractually requested DEHP-free products by Q3 2025.

DESCRIPTION OF CHANGE
  BEFORE: Cable harness assembly (MAT-CABL-HARN, Rev. 3) using PVC insulation compound Evonik Vestolit B7021 (contains DEHP plasticiser, approx. 35 phr)
  AFTER:  Cable harness assembly (MAT-CABL-HARN, Rev. 4) using DEHP-free PVC insulation compound INEOS Lacovyl PB 1702 H (phthalate-free, DINCH plasticiser)

Affected cables within harness:
  – Internal power cables (2.5 mm², brown/blue/green-yellow): all replaced
  – Communication cables (CAT5e, 0.22 mm²): already phthalate-free, no change
  – Pilot signal cables (0.75 mm², grey): replaced

QUALIFICATION EVIDENCE
  Material test report: CCA-QT-2025-012 (tensile strength, elongation, Izod impact, flame resistance UL 94 V-0 – all pass)
  Cable temperature rating: -25°C to +90°C ✓ (no change from previous compound)
  REACH / RoHS compliance declaration: Issued by INEOS (April 2025), sighted ✓
  First article inspection (FAI): FAI-A6-2025-003, completed 18 April 2025, PASS
  Fit-for-purpose validation: 3 harness assemblies built with Rev. 4 cables, installed in test chassis, subjected to 500-cycle mating/unmating test – PASS

IMPACT ASSESSMENT
  Functional impact: NONE – electrical and mechanical performance unchanged
  Safety impact: NONE – flame resistance, temperature rating unchanged
  Cost impact: +EUR 0.85 per harness set (DINCH plasticiser premium vs. DEHP)
  Lead time impact: NONE – Certified Cable Works (A6) has confirmed material in stock
  Documentation updates required: REACH compliance statement (REACH-EVC-2024-001 → Rev. 2), BOM Rev. 4, assembly drawing ASM-CABL-004 Rev. 4

AFFECTED PRODUCT VARIANTS
  AC-7kW, AC-22kW, DC-50kW, DC-150kW – all variants
