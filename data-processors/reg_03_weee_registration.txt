NOTE ON CATEGORY CLASSIFICATION
EV charging stations do not fall neatly within the historical WEEE category definitions. Following guidance from EAR and consultation with VDE Institute, the product is classified under Category 9 (Monitoring and control instruments) as the primary classification, with the power electronics sub-system noted as containing elements of Category 5. This classification is subject to revision pending the European Commission's delegated act on EEE categorisation expected in 2025.
